How to Use the Scientific Calculator

Enter numbers and mathematical operators using the calculator keypad or your computer keyboard. You can combine arithmetic operations with parentheses, powers, roots, logarithms, trigonometric functions, and constants.

  1. Choose DEG for degrees or RAD for radians.
  2. Enter your mathematical expression.
  3. Use scientific functions such as sin, cos, tan, log, ln, √, and x² when needed.
  4. Press Calculate or the Enter key to calculate the result.
  5. Use the memory buttons when you need to temporarily store a number.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a scientific calculator?

A scientific calculator is designed to perform both basic arithmetic and advanced mathematical operations such as trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots, and factorials.

What is the difference between DEG and RAD?

DEG means degrees and RAD means radians. These modes determine how trigonometric functions such as sine, cosine, and tangent interpret angles.

Can I use the calculator with my keyboard?

Yes. You can enter numbers and common arithmetic operators using your keyboard. Enter calculates the expression, Backspace removes the last character, and Escape clears the calculator.

Is this scientific calculator free?

Yes. CalclyWorld's scientific calculator is free to use online without installing a separate application.

Can I calculate trigonometric functions?

Yes. The calculator includes sine, cosine, tangent, and their inverse functions. You can switch between degree and radian modes.
