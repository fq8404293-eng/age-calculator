What Is Unit Price?

Unit price is the cost of a product for one standard unit of quantity. It allows you to compare products that are sold in different package sizes.

For example, one package may cost more overall but contain significantly more product. Looking only at the package price can make comparisons misleading. Unit price provides a more useful way to compare the actual cost.

The basic calculation is:

Unit Price = Total Price ÷ Total Quantity

How to Use the Unit Price Calculator

  1. Enter the product name if you want to identify the item in the result.
  2. Enter the total price of the product.
  3. Enter the total quantity in the package.
  4. Select the appropriate unit.
  5. Click Calculate Unit Price.

The calculator will show the cost per unit and provide a clear summary of the calculation.

Why Compare Unit Prices?

Unit prices make it easier to compare products when package sizes are different. A larger package is not automatically the better value, and a lower shelf price does not always mean a lower cost per unit.

Comparing unit prices can help when shopping for food, cleaning supplies, personal care products, office supplies, and other regularly purchased goods.

The comparison becomes especially useful when two products have different package weights, volumes, or quantities.

Unit Price Examples

Suppose a package costs $6 and contains 12 ounces. The unit price is:

$6 ÷ 12 oz = $0.50 per ounce

Another package may cost $8 and contain 16 ounces. Its unit price is:

$8 ÷ 16 oz = $0.50 per ounce

Although the second package costs more at checkout, both products have the same price per ounce in this example.

Unit Price Formula

The unit price formula is straightforward:

Unit Price = Total Price ÷ Quantity

For example, if a product costs $10 and contains 20 units:

$10 ÷ 20 = $0.50 per unit

The same principle can be used for weight, volume, or individual items as long as the quantity and unit are clearly defined.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a unit price?

Unit price is the cost of one unit of a product. It helps compare products with different package sizes.

How do you calculate unit price?

Divide the total price by the total quantity. For example, $12 divided by 6 items equals $2 per item.

Why is unit price useful?

Unit price makes it easier to determine which product offers better value when packages have different sizes or quantities.

Can I compare different package sizes?

Yes. Unit pricing is specifically useful for comparing products sold in different quantities.

Does a larger package always have a lower unit price?

No. A larger package may cost more per unit. Always compare the calculated unit prices rather than assuming a larger package is cheaper.
