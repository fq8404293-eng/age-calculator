How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator finds the amount of time between your date of birth and today's date. Instead of simply subtracting birth years, it considers the month and day as well, giving you a more precise age.

What Can an Age Calculator Tell You?

  • Your exact age in years, months and days
  • Your total number of days lived
  • Your total weeks, months and hours
  • The weekday on which you were born
  • Your next birthday date and countdown
  • Your zodiac sign and birthstone

Why Is Exact Age Different From Simply Subtracting Years?

For example, someone born near the end of a year may not have reached their next birthday yet. An exact age calculation checks both the month and day so that the result reflects whether the birthday has already occurred.

Does the Calculator Handle Leap Years?

Yes. The calculation accounts for the different number of days in each month and leap years when determining the elapsed time between dates.
