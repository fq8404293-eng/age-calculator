What Is Car Depreciation?

Car depreciation is the reduction in a vehicle's value over time. Most vehicles lose value as they become older and accumulate mileage, although the amount of depreciation varies between vehicles.

Depreciation is important when buying, selling, financing, or owning a vehicle because the vehicle's market value can be significantly different from the price originally paid.

This calculator compares the original purchase price with the current estimated vehicle value to show how much value has been lost.

How to Calculate Car Depreciation

The simplest way to calculate depreciation is to subtract the current vehicle value from the original purchase price.

Total Depreciation Formula

Depreciation = Original Price − Current Value

The percentage of value lost can then be calculated by dividing the depreciation amount by the original purchase price.

Depreciation Percentage Formula

Depreciation % = (Depreciation ÷ Original Price) × 100

If the ownership period is known, the calculator can also estimate the average depreciation per year.

Average Annual Depreciation

Average Annual Depreciation = Total Depreciation ÷ Ownership Years

Factors That Affect Car Depreciation

Vehicle depreciation is not determined by age alone. Several factors can influence how much a vehicle is worth in the used-car market.

  • Vehicle age
  • Mileage and usage
  • Make and model
  • Overall mechanical condition
  • Exterior and interior condition
  • Service and maintenance history
  • Accident or damage history
  • Demand for the vehicle
  • Number of previous owners
  • Local used-vehicle market conditions

Popular models with strong used-market demand may retain their value better than vehicles with weaker demand. Actual resale value can also vary significantly between regions and markets.

Why Car Depreciation Matters

Depreciation is an important part of the true cost of owning a vehicle. Two vehicles can have similar fuel, insurance, and maintenance expenses while experiencing very different losses in market value.

Understanding depreciation can help when comparing vehicles, deciding whether to buy new or used, estimating resale value, or evaluating the long-term cost of ownership.

The calculator provides a simple historical depreciation estimate based on the values you enter. It should not be treated as a guaranteed prediction of future resale value.

Car Depreciation FAQ

What is car depreciation?

Car depreciation is the decrease in a vehicle's market value over time. It represents the difference between what the vehicle was worth or purchased for and what it is worth later.

How is car depreciation calculated?

A simple depreciation calculation subtracts the current value from the original purchase price. The resulting loss can then be expressed as a percentage of the original price.

Does every car depreciate at the same rate?

No. Depreciation varies by vehicle. Age, mileage, condition, demand, reliability, brand reputation, and local market conditions can all influence resale value.

Can I use this calculator for a motorcycle?

Yes. Although the calculator is designed around vehicle depreciation, the same basic calculation can be used for motorcycles and other vehicles when comparing their original and current values.

Does this calculator predict future car value?

No. This version calculates depreciation from an original price and a current estimated value. Future resale prices depend on market conditions and cannot be guaranteed from a simple historical calculation.

What is average annual depreciation?

Average annual depreciation is the total value lost divided by the number of years the vehicle has been owned. It is a simple average and does not mean that a vehicle loses exactly the same amount every year.
