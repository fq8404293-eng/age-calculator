What Is Recipe Scaling?

Recipe scaling means adjusting ingredient quantities so a recipe produces a different number of servings than the original recipe.

For example, if a recipe makes 4 servings and you want to make 8 servings, the ingredient quantities generally need to be doubled. If you want only 2 servings, the ingredient quantities generally need to be reduced by half.

The calculator applies the same scaling factor to each ingredient quantity you enter while keeping the original measurement unit.

How to Use the Recipe Converter

  1. Enter the number of servings in the original recipe.
  2. Enter the number of servings you want to prepare.
  3. Enter the ingredient name.
  4. Enter the original quantity and measurement unit.
  5. Add up to five ingredients.
  6. Click Convert Recipe.

The calculator will multiply each ingredient quantity by the scaling factor needed to reach your desired serving size.

Understanding the Scaling Factor

The scaling factor tells you how much each ingredient quantity needs to change.

A factor of 2 means the ingredient quantity is doubled. A factor of 0.5 means the quantity is reduced to half of the original amount.

For best results, check measurements carefully when working with very small quantities such as spices, flavorings, or leavening ingredients.

Recipe Scaling Tips

  • Use the same measurement unit you normally use in the recipe.
  • Be careful when scaling very small ingredient quantities.
  • Cooking time may not increase proportionally when a recipe is scaled up.
  • Pan or baking-dish size may need to be changed when preparing a significantly larger batch.
  • For baking, large changes in recipe size may require additional adjustments beyond simply multiplying every ingredient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I double a recipe?

Yes. Enter the original number of servings and double the desired servings. The calculator will multiply the ingredient quantities by the resulting scaling factor.

Can I reduce a recipe?

Yes. Enter a smaller desired serving size than the original recipe. The calculator will reduce each ingredient quantity accordingly.

Does the calculator convert cups to grams?

No. This calculator scales the numerical quantity while keeping the measurement unit you enter. Weight-to-volume conversions depend on the specific ingredient.

Can I scale baking recipes?

You can use the calculator to scale the ingredient quantities, but large changes to baking recipes may require additional adjustments to pan size, cooking time, temperature, and other baking variables.

How many ingredients can I enter?

This calculator allows you to enter up to five ingredients at one time.
