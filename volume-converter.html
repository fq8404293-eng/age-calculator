What Is a Volume Converter?

A volume converter changes a measurement from one volume unit into another equivalent unit. This is useful when working with cooking measurements, beverages, fuel, liquids, containers, construction materials, science, and everyday measurements.

CalclyWorld's Volume Converter supports several commonly used metric and US customary units, allowing you to compare measurements without performing the conversion manually.

Volume Units Supported

Liter L A standard metric unit of volume.

How Volume Conversion Works

The calculator first converts the entered value into a common base volume and then converts that base value into each supported unit.

For example, one liter is equal to 1,000 milliliters. Therefore, converting liters to milliliters involves multiplying the number of liters by 1,000.

Example: 2 liters × 1,000 = 2,000 milliliters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many milliliters are in one liter?

One liter contains 1,000 milliliters.

How many liters are in a US gallon?

One US gallon equals approximately 3.78541 liters.

Is a cubic centimeter the same as a milliliter?

Yes. One cubic centimeter (cm³) has the same volume as one milliliter (mL).

Does this calculator use US gallons?

Yes. The gallon, quart, pint, cup, and fluid-ounce conversions on this page use US customary liquid-volume units.
