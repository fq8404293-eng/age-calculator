What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred," and the symbol % is used to represent it.

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, price changes, grades, statistics, financial calculations, taxes, interest, business reports, and everyday comparisons.

This Percentage Calculator provides three common calculations: finding a percentage of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, and calculating percentage increase or decrease.

Percentage Formula

The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you have 25 out of 100:

(25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%

Percentage Examples

Example 1: What is 20% of 150?

Multiply 20 by 150 and divide by 100.

(20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30

Example 2: 30 is what percentage of 150?

Divide 30 by 150 and multiply by 100.

(30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%

Example 3: A price increases from $100 to $125

The increase is $25. Divide $25 by the original $100 and multiply by 100.

(25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%

Example 4: A price decreases from $100 to $75

The decrease is $25. Divide $25 by the original $100 and multiply by 100.

(25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%

Reverse Percentage Calculations

Sometimes you know the final amount and the percentage, but need to find the original amount.

Finding the Original Value After an Increase

Original = Final ÷ (1 + Increase ÷ 100)

Finding the Original Value After a Decrease

Original = Final ÷ (1 − Decrease ÷ 100)

These formulas can be useful for working backward from discounted prices, increased prices, or other percentage-based changes.

Common Percentage Mistakes

  • Using the wrong reference value when calculating percentage change.
  • Forgetting to divide by 100 when calculating a percentage of a number.
  • Confusing percentage change with the difference between two percentages.
  • Using the new value instead of the original value as the reference when calculating a percentage increase or decrease.
  • Assuming a percentage increase and the same percentage decrease cancel each other out.

Percentage vs Percentage Points

Percentage and percentage points describe different things.

For example, if an interest rate changes from 5% to 7%, the difference is 2 percentage points.

The relative percentage increase, however, is:

((7 − 5) ÷ 5) × 100 = 40%

Therefore, the rate increased by 2 percentage points, which is a 40% relative increase.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage expresses a number as a fraction of 100.

How do I calculate X% of a number?

Multiply the percentage by the number and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.

Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. A percentage can be greater than 100% when the value being compared is greater than the reference value.

What is the difference between percentage and percentage points?

Percentage points describe the arithmetic difference between two percentage values, while percentage change describes the relative change.
