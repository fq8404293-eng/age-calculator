What Is an Age Difference?

Age difference is the amount of time between two people's dates of birth or between any two dates. Depending on the dates you enter, the difference can be expressed in years, months, and days.

An age difference calculator is useful when comparing the ages of two people, checking the difference between important dates, or simply understanding how much time separates two dates.

How to Use the Age Difference Calculator

  1. Enter the first date.
  2. Enter the second date.
  3. Click Calculate Age Difference.
  4. Review the difference in years, months, and days.

You can enter the dates in either order. The calculator compares the two dates and determines the absolute difference between them.

Age Difference Example

Suppose one person was born on January 15, 1995, and another person was born on June 20, 2000.

The calculator determines the exact difference between these dates and presents the result in years, months, and days.

This provides a more precise result than simply subtracting the birth years because the months and days are also taken into account.

Important Notes

  • The calculator uses the actual calendar dates you enter.
  • Leap years are taken into account when calculating the difference.
  • The order of the two dates does not affect the absolute age difference.
  • The result is different from simply dividing the total number of days by 365 because calendar months and leap years vary in length.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compare the ages of two people?

Yes. Enter the two people's dates of birth to calculate the exact difference between their ages.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. Calendar dates and leap years are considered when calculating the difference.

Do I need to enter the older date first?

No. You can enter the dates in either order. The calculator determines the difference between them.

Can I use this for dates other than birthdays?

Yes. You can use the calculator to compare any two calendar dates.
