What Is an Average?

An average, also called the arithmetic mean, is a way of finding a single representative value from a group of numbers. It is commonly used to summarize data and compare values.

To calculate an average, add all the numbers together and then divide the total by the number of values in the group.

Average Formula

The arithmetic mean is calculated using the following formula:

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

For example, if your numbers are 10, 20, and 30, their sum is 60. There are 3 values, so the average is 60 ÷ 3 = 20.

Average Calculation Example

Suppose you have the following five numbers:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

First, add the numbers together:

10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150

There are 5 numbers, so:

150 ÷ 5 = 30

Therefore, the average is 30.

Where Is an Average Used?

Averages are useful in many everyday and professional situations. They can help summarize a collection of numbers into one easy-to- understand value.

  • Calculating average test scores
  • Analyzing monthly expenses
  • Comparing average sales
  • Reviewing average temperatures
  • Analyzing business performance
  • Summarizing numerical data

Important Note About Averages

The arithmetic mean works best when you want every value in a dataset to contribute equally to the result.

An average can sometimes be affected significantly by very large or very small values. For datasets with extreme values, other measures such as the median may provide additional useful context.
