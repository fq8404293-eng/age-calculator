Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CalclyWorld handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. Introduction

Welcome to CalclyWorld. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you use our website.

CalclyWorld provides free online calculators and related educational information. Most calculators are designed to perform calculations directly in your web browser.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected through the website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to visitors.

By using CalclyWorld, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

CalclyWorld does not require visitors to create an account to use the basic calculators available on the website.

Depending on how you interact with the website and which services are enabled, information may be collected automatically by CalclyWorld or by third-party services used on the website.

Examples may include:

  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type or general device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • General usage information, such as pages visited.
  • Approximate geographic information derived from technical information, where provided by a third-party service.
  • Referring website or general traffic source information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

The exact information collected can vary depending on your browser, device, privacy settings, and third-party services that may be active on the website.

3. Information Entered Into Calculators

Many CalclyWorld calculators are designed to perform calculations directly in your browser.

Information entered into a calculator may therefore be processed locally by the calculator's JavaScript code rather than being submitted to a CalclyWorld database.

However, you should not assume that every website interaction is completely private from third-party technologies. Browser extensions, analytics services, advertising services, network providers, and other technologies may operate independently.

For this reason, avoid entering passwords, bank account numbers, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into calculators unless the calculator specifically requires such information and you understand the associated risks.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information available to CalclyWorld or its service providers may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Improving calculator functionality and usability.
  • Understanding general website usage and performance.
  • Identifying technical problems and errors.
  • Responding to emails and other inquiries.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Measuring advertising performance, where advertising services are enabled.

We do not need your personal information to provide basic access to most CalclyWorld calculators.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

CalclyWorld or third-party services used on the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies.

These technologies can be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, measuring website usage, improving functionality, security, analytics, or displaying and measuring advertisements.

CalclyWorld's dark mode preference, for example, may be stored locally in your browser so that your display preference can be remembered.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect some website features.

6. Analytics

CalclyWorld may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the website and to help improve website performance and content.

Analytics services may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device information, browser information, traffic sources, and interactions with the website.

Analytics providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their data collection and processing may also depend on your browser settings and available privacy controls.

7. Advertising

CalclyWorld may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements, depending on applicable settings, consent requirements, and regional rules.

Third-party advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Where required, appropriate consent or privacy choices may be presented to visitors before certain advertising or measurement technologies are used.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

CalclyWorld may use third-party services to support website functionality, analytics, advertising, security, hosting, or other technical operations.

The website may also contain links to websites operated by other organizations.

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies and terms. CalclyWorld is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that it does not control.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

9. Information You Send Us

If you contact CalclyWorld by email, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, name if provided, and the contents of your inquiry.

This information may be used to understand and respond to your request, investigate reported calculator issues, consider suggestions, or communicate with you regarding your inquiry.

Please do not send passwords, payment card information, bank account numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information by email.

10. Sharing of Information

CalclyWorld does not need to sell your personal information in order to provide access to its basic calculators.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide analytics, deliver advertising, maintain security, respond to requests, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website and information under our control.

However, no website, internet connection, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through the website or by email.

12. Children's Privacy

CalclyWorld is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to CalclyWorld that should not have been provided, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

13. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights or choices concerning personal information, cookies, advertising, analytics, or other data processing.

Available choices may include controlling cookies through your browser, using available consent or privacy controls, or contacting us about information that you have voluntarily provided directly to CalclyWorld.

Some information may be processed directly by third-party services. Requests relating to information controlled by those providers may need to be made directly to the relevant provider.

14. International Visitors

CalclyWorld may be accessed by visitors from different countries. Depending on the services used and your location, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Privacy rights and requirements vary by jurisdiction. Where applicable, CalclyWorld aims to respect relevant privacy requirements and provide appropriate choices.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically for the latest information.
