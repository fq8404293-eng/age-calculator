What Is a Time Zone Converter?

A time zone converter changes a date and time from one geographic time zone to the corresponding local time in another time zone.

Time zones are important because different parts of the world use different local times. Some locations also change their clocks seasonally because of daylight saving time.

A time zone converter makes it easier to coordinate meetings, calls, travel plans, online events, and international work schedules.

How to Use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Select the date you want to convert.
  2. Select the hour, minute, and AM/PM period.
  3. Select the original time zone.
  4. Select the destination time zone.
  5. Click Convert Time.

The calculator displays the converted local date and time in the destination time zone.

Why Time Zone Conversion Matters

Scheduling an event across different countries can be confusing because the same moment can have a different local clock time in each location.

For example, a business meeting scheduled in New York may occur at a different local hour in London, Mumbai, Tokyo, or Sydney.

Time zone conversion helps prevent scheduling mistakes by showing the equivalent local time for the selected destination.

Daylight Saving Time

Some time zones observe daylight saving time, while others remain on the same standard offset throughout the year.

Because of this, the difference between two locations can change during the year. This calculator uses time zone rules provided by the browser to account for applicable daylight saving changes.

Time Zone Conversion Examples

Suppose a meeting is scheduled for 9:00 AM in New York. You can select New York as the source time zone and another location such as London or Mumbai as the destination.

The calculator then shows the corresponding local date and time for the selected destination.

If the conversion crosses midnight, the result will also show the appropriate date in the destination time zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a time zone converter do?

It converts a specific date and time from one time zone into the equivalent local date and time in another time zone.

Does it account for daylight saving time?

Yes. The calculator uses the selected time zones' built-in browser time zone rules, including applicable daylight saving changes.

Can the converted date change?

Yes. If the time difference crosses midnight, the destination location may have a different calendar date.

Can I convert between US and international time zones?

Yes. The calculator includes several US, European, Asian, Australian, African, and other international time zones.

What happens if I leave the minute blank?

An empty minute selection is treated as 00 minutes, so you only need to select a minute when you want something other than the hour.
