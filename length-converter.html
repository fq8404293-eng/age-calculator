Metric and Imperial Length Units

Length can be measured using different systems around the world. The metric system uses units such as millimeters, centimeters, meters and kilometers, while the imperial system commonly uses inches, feet, yards and miles.

Unit Symbol Relationship
Millimeter mm 1 mm = 0.001 m
Centimeter cm 1 cm = 0.01 m
Meter m Base metric length unit
Kilometer km 1 km = 1,000 m
Inch in 1 in = 25.4 mm
Foot ft 1 ft = 12 in
Yard yd 1 yd = 3 ft
Mile mi 1 mi = 5,280 ft

Length Conversion Formula

The calculator first converts the entered measurement into meters. It then converts the meter value into every supported length unit.

1 inch = 0.0254 meters

1 foot = 0.3048 meters

1 yard = 0.9144 meters

1 mile = 1,609.344 meters

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters

Length Conversion Example

Suppose you want to convert 10 feet into metric units.

10 ft = 3.048 m

10 ft = 304.8 cm

10 ft = 3,048 mm

The same 10 feet is approximately 3.3333 yards and 0.001864 miles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in a meter?

One meter equals approximately 3.28084 feet.

How many centimeters are in an inch?

One inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters.

How many kilometers are in a mile?

One international mile equals exactly 1.609344 kilometers.

How many inches are in a foot?

One foot contains exactly 12 inches.

How many feet are in a yard?

One yard contains exactly 3 feet.

What is the difference between metric and imperial units?

Metric units are based on powers of ten, while commonly used imperial units include inches, feet, yards and miles.
