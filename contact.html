Reporting a Calculator Problem

If you are reporting a calculation problem, including a few details in your email can make the issue much easier to investigate.

  1. Tell us the name of the calculator.
  2. Tell us the values you entered.
  3. Tell us the result you received.
  4. Explain what you expected or what appears incorrect.
  5. If relevant, mention the device or browser you were using.

Please do not include passwords, bank account numbers, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information in your message.
