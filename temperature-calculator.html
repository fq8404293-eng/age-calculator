Temperature Scales

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin are three commonly encountered temperature scales. Celsius is widely used for everyday temperature measurements, Fahrenheit is commonly used in the United States, and Kelvin is widely used in scientific work.

Scale Symbol Common Reference
Celsius °C Water freezes at 0°C
Fahrenheit °F Water freezes at 32°F
Kelvin K Absolute temperature scale

Temperature Conversion Formulas

Temperature scales use different zero points and intervals, so converting temperatures requires formulas rather than simply multiplying by a constant.

Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32

Celsius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5/9

Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15

Celsius = Kelvin − 273.15

Temperature Conversion Example

Suppose the temperature is 25°C.

25°C = 77°F

25°C = 298.15 K

The Fahrenheit value is calculated as (25 × 9/5) + 32 = 77°F.

Where Temperature Conversion Is Useful

Temperature conversion is useful when reading weather reports, cooking instructions, scientific measurements, equipment specifications and information from countries that use different temperature scales.

Situation Common Temperature Scale
Weather reports Celsius or Fahrenheit
Cooking Celsius or Fahrenheit
Scientific calculations Kelvin
Laboratory measurements Kelvin or Celsius
International specifications Celsius, Kelvin or Fahrenheit

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and then add 32. For example, 25°C equals 77°F.

How do you convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature and multiply the result by 5/9.

How do you convert Celsius to Kelvin?

Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature. For example, 25°C equals 298.15 K.

Is Kelvin the same as Celsius?

No. The Celsius and Kelvin scales have the same size temperature interval, but their zero points are different. A temperature of 0°C equals 273.15 K.

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 K, which corresponds to −273.15°C or −459.67°F.

What temperature is 100°C in Fahrenheit?

100°C equals 212°F.
