What Is Currency Exchange?

Currency exchange is the process of converting one country's currency into another currency. The exchange rate determines how much of the second currency is received for a given amount of the first currency.

Exchange rates can change over time because currency markets respond to economic conditions, interest rates, inflation, international trade, and changes in market demand.

A currency converter can help you estimate the value of money when planning a trip, comparing international prices, or preparing a personal budget.

Understanding Currency Exchange Rates

An exchange rate shows the value of one currency relative to another. For example, a rate of 1.10 EUR per USD means that one US dollar is equivalent to approximately 1.10 euros at that quoted rate.

The rate displayed by a currency calculator may differ from the rate offered by a bank, credit card company, airport exchange counter, or money-transfer service.

Financial institutions may add transaction fees, foreign exchange fees, or a conversion markup. These charges can change the final amount received or paid.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an exchange rate?

An exchange rate is the value of one currency expressed in another currency.

Why do exchange rates change?

Exchange rates can change because of supply and demand, economic conditions, interest rates, inflation, trade, and market expectations.

Will my bank use the same exchange rate?

Not necessarily. Banks and payment providers can use their own exchange rates and may add fees or conversion markups.

Can I use this calculator for travel?

Yes. It can help you estimate the value of your money in another currency while planning travel expenses.

Is the calculated amount guaranteed?

No. Currency rates can change, and the final amount may differ because of provider rates and additional fees.
