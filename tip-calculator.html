What Is a Tip?

A tip, also called gratuity, is an additional amount of money given to a service worker to recognize the service provided. Restaurant tipping practices vary by country, business, and situation.

A tip is generally calculated as a percentage of the bill. For example, a 15% tip on a $100 bill is $15, making the total $115.

How to Calculate a Tip

The basic tip formula is:

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

For example, if your bill is $80 and you choose a 20% tip:

$80 × 20 ÷ 100 = $16

The total bill would therefore be:

$80 + $16 = $96

How to Split a Bill

When several people share a bill, the total amount including the tip can be divided equally among the number of people.

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

For example, if a bill including tip is $120 and four people are sharing it equally:

$120 ÷ 4 = $30 per person

Tipping Practices Vary by Location

Tipping customs are not the same everywhere. In some countries, tipping is common in restaurants and other service industries, while in other countries service charges or different payment customs are more common.

Before deciding how much to tip, consider the local customs, the restaurant's policies, and whether a service charge has already been included in the bill.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% tip?

Multiply the bill amount by 0.20. For example, a $100 bill would have a $20 tip at 20%.

Can I split the bill between multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of people sharing the bill and the calculator will show the total amount and amount per person.

Does the calculator include the tip in the total?

Yes. The total bill includes the original bill amount plus the calculated tip.

Can I enter a custom tip percentage?

Yes. You can enter any tip percentage from 0% to 100%.

Does the calculator require a currency?

No. The calculator works with the numerical value of your bill, so you can use it with dollars, euros, pounds, rupees, or another currency. The result uses the same currency as your input.
