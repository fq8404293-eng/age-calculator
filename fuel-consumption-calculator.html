What Is Fuel Consumption?

Fuel consumption describes how much fuel a vehicle uses to travel a particular distance. It is commonly expressed as liters per 100 kilometers (L/100 km), while fuel economy may be expressed as kilometers per liter (km/L) or miles per gallon (MPG).

The amount of fuel a vehicle consumes depends on factors such as engine efficiency, vehicle weight, speed, traffic, road conditions, tire pressure, weather, and driving style.

Fuel Consumption Formulas

Fuel Economy (km/L) = Distance (km) ÷ Fuel Used (L)

Fuel Consumption (L/100 km) = Fuel Used (L) ÷ Distance (km) × 100

MPG = Miles Traveled ÷ US Gallons Used

The calculator converts your entered distance and fuel quantity into compatible units before calculating the different fuel-efficiency measurements.

Understanding Fuel Economy Measurements

Kilometers per Liter (km/L)

km/L tells you how many kilometers a vehicle can travel using one liter of fuel. A higher km/L number generally means better fuel economy.

Liters per 100 Kilometers (L/100 km)

L/100 km tells you how many liters of fuel a vehicle uses to travel 100 kilometers. Unlike km/L, a lower L/100 km number indicates better fuel efficiency.

Miles per Gallon (MPG)

MPG measures how many miles a vehicle can travel using one US gallon of fuel. A higher MPG value generally means better fuel economy.

Tips for Measuring Real-World Fuel Economy

For a useful real-world estimate, record the distance traveled and fuel added over several trips instead of relying on a single short journey.

Keeping tire pressure at the recommended level and avoiding unnecessary vehicle weight can help maintain efficient fuel consumption.

Comparing fuel economy under similar driving conditions gives a more meaningful picture of vehicle efficiency.

Fuel Consumption Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate fuel consumption?

Divide the amount of fuel used by the distance traveled and multiply by 100 when calculating L/100 km. The calculator performs the conversion automatically.

What is the difference between km/L and L/100 km?

km/L measures how far a vehicle travels per liter, so higher values are better. L/100 km measures how much fuel is needed for 100 kilometers, so lower values are better.

Can I calculate MPG?

Yes. Enter your distance in miles and fuel used in US gallons to calculate MPG.

Does higher MPG mean better fuel economy?

Yes. A higher MPG value means the vehicle can travel farther using the same amount of fuel.

Does lower L/100 km mean better fuel economy?

Yes. A lower L/100 km value means the vehicle uses less fuel to travel 100 kilometers.

Why can my real-world fuel economy differ?

Actual fuel economy can vary because of traffic, speed, weather, road conditions, vehicle load, tire pressure, maintenance, and driving style.
