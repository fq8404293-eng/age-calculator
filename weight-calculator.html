Metric and US Customary Weight Units

Weight measurements are commonly expressed using metric units such as grams and kilograms, or US customary units such as pounds and ounces. This converter lets you compare these units easily.

Unit Symbol Relationship

Gram g 1 g = 0.001 kg
Kilogram kg Base metric unit used for everyday weight
Metric Ton t 1 t = 1,000 kg
Ounce oz 1 oz ≈ 28.3495 g
Pound lb 1 lb = 16 oz
Stone st 1 st = 14 lb

Weight Conversion Formula

Weight Conversion Example

Suppose you want to convert 70 kilograms into pounds and ounces.

70 kg ≈ 154.3236 lb

70 kg ≈ 2,469.1773 oz

The same 70 kg is equal to 70,000 grams and 0.07 metric tons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pounds are in 1 kilogram?

One kilogram equals approximately 2.20462 pounds.

How many kilograms are in 1 pound?

One pound equals exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

How many grams are in 1 kilogram?

One kilogram contains exactly 1,000 grams.

How many ounces are in 1 pound?

One pound contains exactly 16 ounces.

How many grams are in 1 ounce?

One avoirdupois ounce equals approximately 28.3495 grams.

What is the difference between kg and lb?

A kilogram is a metric unit, while a pound is a US customary and imperial unit. One kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds.
